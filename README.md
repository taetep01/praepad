# 💖 Happy 2 Years & 5 Months - Anniversary Website

A cute, interactive, romantic pink-themed anniversary web application designed for celebrating **2 Years & 5 Months** together! Fully responsive across **Mobile (iPhone / Android)**, **iPad / Tablets**, and **Desktop**.

---

## ✨ Features Included

1. **🌸 Cute Pink & Pastel Aesthetic**:
   - Soft rosy hues, frosted glassmorphism, bouncy micro-interactions, and floating hearts/sparkles.
   - Beautiful Google typography (`Fredoka`, `Nunito`, `Sacramento`).

2. **⏳ Live Love Milestone Counter**:
   - Real-time live counter showing **Years, Months, Days, Hours, Minutes, and Seconds**.
   - Calculates total days of happiness and total heartbeats spent together.

3. **💌 Interactive Wax-Seal Love Letter**:
   - Tap the wax seal to open the envelope and slide out a heartfelt customizable letter.
   - Includes a built-in "✏️ Edit Letter" button to customize your personal words anytime.

4. **✨ Jar of Little Reasons I Love You**:
   - Interactive candy glass jar filled with cute floating capsules.
   - Tap to pull one of 35+ heartwarming, funny, and romantic reasons why you love her.

5. **📸 Polaroid Scrapbook Memories**:
   - Vintage polaroid cards with cute washi tapes.
   - **Interactive Photo Upload**: Tap any photo card to instantly select and replace it with your real couple photos right from your phone or iPad (saved automatically to your browser!).

6. **🎁 Scratch-to-Reveal Love Coupons**:
   - 4 interactive scratch cards with touch/swipe support:
     - 👑 *Princess For A Day (Pampering & Massages)*
     - 🍦 *Midnight Dessert & Boba Run*
     - 🎬 *Movie Night Choice Pass*
     - ✈️ *Surprise Date Trip Planned by Me*

7. **🧸 "Our Love Quiz" Mini Game**:
   - Cute interactive trivia with sweet reactions and confetti rewards.

8. **🎵 Romantic Music Box Synthesizer**:
   - Built-in soothing dreamy lullaby chime player powered by the browser's Web Audio API (works offline without needing any MP3 downloads).

9. **⚙️ Quick Customization (Settings Modal)**:
   - Tap the ⚙️ icon at the top right to customize your girlfriend's nickname, your name, anniversary start date, and subtitle.

## 🚀 How to View & Share

### Option 1: Open Locally (Immediate)
- Simply double-click [`index.html`](file:///D:/2year5months/index.html) in any browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox).

### Option 2: Host for Free & Send the Link to Her Phone/iPad
You can put this online in under 1 minute for free:
- **Netlify Drop**: Drag and drop the `2year5months` folder into [app.netlify.com/drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop) to get a free live URL (e.g. `our-love-story.netlify.app`).
- **GitHub Pages**: Push this folder to a GitHub repository and turn on GitHub Pages in the repository settings.
- **Vercel**: Deploy with one click or command `npx vercel`.
